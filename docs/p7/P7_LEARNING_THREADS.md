# P7 Learning Threads

A learning thread is an owned, ordered sequence of questions, concepts,
evidence, and practice artifacts. It has `active`, `completed`, and `archived`
states and can be resumed without exposing unrelated personal history.

Thread guidance is evidence-linked. It may propose a review item when a
concept is `NEEDS_REVIEW`, a transfer question after repeated exposure, or a
research task when a limitation is unresolved. It must state the evidence ids
and limitations that motivated the suggestion. The guidance layer cannot
change a P5/P6 quant result, promote a claim to FACT, or place a trade.

The repository stores thread membership and position, while the product layer
renders a timeline and next action. Stale source fingerprints mark dependent
projections stale; they do not silently rewrite a learning history entry.
