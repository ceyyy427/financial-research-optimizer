# P7 Strategy History

Strategy history links reviewed specifications, IR fingerprints, feature
graphs, backtest/OOS metadata, paper runs, learning cards, and limitations.
It reuses P6.6's compiler and backtest authorities rather than recalculating a
private approximation. Each link carries a source fingerprint and provenance
commit so a later review can reproduce the result.

The history is descriptive, not advisory. It may show what was tested and what
changed, but it cannot rank investments, infer a future return, or invoke a
broker. `paper=True` remains simulation-only; live execution is outside P7.
