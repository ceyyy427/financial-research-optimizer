# P7 Evidence-Driven Community Model

Community is organized into rooms with explicit membership. Posts carry a
claim label (`FACT`, `INTERPRETATION`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `QUANT_FINDING`, `UNKNOWN`,
or `LIMITATION`) and may attach only an active shared/public projection. A
projection is a sanitized view, never a pointer that grants access to its
private source.

Members can ask questions, comment, save a post as a private question, and
link evidence. The post author controls attachments in the first slice;
moderation can remove room content but cannot read private graphs. Public
content remains subject to revocation and stale-source checks.

The community contract separates discussion from truth. A claim label and
evidence link make uncertainty visible; labels are not endorsements and public
visibility is not a validity upgrade. Harassment, prompt injection, secrets,
and trading instructions are out of scope for publication.
