# P7 Personalization Boundary

Personalization changes sequence, examples, pacing, and reminders. It does
not change truth, evidence status, validity labels, source limitations,
financial safety language, or the authority of P5/P6/P6.5/P6.6 outputs.

Allowed: choose a next learning thread from the owner's private open items,
reuse a previously saved definition, explain a known misconception, or ask a
transfer question at an appropriate difficulty. Not allowed: hide a contrary
result because it conflicts with a user's preference, convert a hypothesis to
fact, fabricate a source, expose another user's graph, or personalize a
recommendation to buy/sell.

Every guidance action has a purpose and bounded context. Users can inspect the
evidence references and revoke projection consent; a revoked projection is not
reintroduced by a recommendation cache.
