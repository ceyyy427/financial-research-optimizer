# Code of Conduct

We expect contributors to act with respect, care, and intellectual honesty.
Discuss evidence and assumptions directly, welcome correction, and credit
other people's work. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and
deliberate misrepresentation of research are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject changes, or restrict
participation when conduct conflicts with this code. Report concerns privately
to the project maintainers with enough context to investigate.
