# Code of Conduct

We expect contributors to act with respect, care, and intellectual honesty.
Discuss evidence and assumptions directly, welcome correction, and credit
other people's work. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions,
documentation, community projections, and private maintainer channels.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
doxxing, threats, deliberate misrepresentation of research, publishing
someone else's private data, and using the project to solicit or promise
financial outcomes. Good-faith corrections and disagreement about evidence are
welcome; personal attacks are not.

If you experience or witness a problem, contact the maintainers privately with
the relevant links, dates, participants, and any safe evidence. Do not include
secrets or private research. Maintainers will protect confidentiality where
possible, investigate impartially, and may remove content, reject changes,
restrict participation, or refer serious threats to the appropriate service.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant principles and is intended
to make research collaboration safe, accountable, and open to correction.
